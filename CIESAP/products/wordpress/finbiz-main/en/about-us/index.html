WHO WE ARE

A Burkinabè Consulting and Services Company

CIESAP Burkina SARL supports businesses, organizations, projects, institutions, and economic stakeholders in designing and implementing solutions that create value and impact. Our approach combines technical expertise, an understanding of the local context, a results orientation, and the capacity to deliver.

Our Vision

To become a Burkinabè and West African reference in consulting, innovation, SME support, and impact-driven solutions.

Our Mission

To turn expertise into useful, measurable, and lasting solutions that improve organizational performance and strengthen economic opportunities.
